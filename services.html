IT Assessment & Prioritization

Understand the environment before committing to changes.

  • Review infrastructure, Microsoft 365, networking, security and backup
  • Identify operational, security and continuity risks
  • Clarify recurring issues and ownership gaps
  • Review vendor dependencies, licensing and key technology constraints
  • Prioritize urgent risks versus planned improvements
  • Define practical next steps and an improvement roadmap
Best for businesses that need a clear picture of current risks and priorities before starting a project or ongoing engagement.

Fractional IT Management

Senior IT leadership without a full-time senior hire.

  • Technology planning and prioritization
  • IT policies, procedures and operational controls
  • Vendor and supplier coordination
  • Budget and licensing oversight
  • Issue escalation and management follow-up
  • Technology roadmap and modernization planning
Best for businesses that need consistent senior IT ownership and decision support without adding a full-time IT manager.

Microsoft 365

Secure, organized and properly managed Microsoft 365.

  • Microsoft 365 administration and user lifecycle management
  • Exchange Online administration, mail-flow configuration, retention, security and Outlook support
  • Licensing review and optimization
  • Identity, access and permissions
  • Security settings and practical hardening
  • Collaboration and workflow improvement
Best for organizations that rely heavily on email, collaboration and cloud services but need stronger administration and control.

Infrastructure & Networking

Reliable systems, connectivity and performance.

  • Network and infrastructure assessment
  • Servers, virtualization and system administration
  • LAN, Wi-Fi, routing, switching and firewall coordination
  • Infrastructure modernization planning
  • Network, server and system performance assessment, bottleneck identification and improvement planning
  • Technical troubleshooting and root-cause follow-up
Best for businesses dealing with unreliable connectivity, aging infrastructure, recurring technical issues or upcoming upgrades.

Cybersecurity

Practical controls that reduce operational and security risk.

  • Access-control and user-permission review
  • Firewall and endpoint-security coordination
  • Security hardening and operational controls
  • Monitoring and incident-response readiness
  • Security-policy improvement
  • Vendor and solution evaluation
Best for businesses that need stronger protection and governance without turning every security decision into a complex enterprise project.

Backup & Business Continuity

Prepare the business to recover when systems fail.

  • Backup strategy and coverage review
  • Recovery planning and recoverability checks
  • Disaster-recovery planning
  • Business-continuity measures for critical systems
  • Redundancy and availability recommendations
  • Recovery priorities and operational documentation
Best for businesses where data loss, prolonged downtime or a failed recovery would directly affect operations.

IT Operations & Team Oversight

Strengthen existing IT capability with senior oversight.

  • Operational priorities and management escalation
  • Support for internal IT staff on complex changes and projects
  • Vendor and service-provider coordination
  • Procedures, documentation and ownership clarification
  • Root-cause follow-up for recurring operational issues
  • Planning and management communication
Best for businesses that already have internal IT capability but need additional senior guidance, escalation support or project leadership.

IT Projects & Vendor Management

Better coordination from requirements through implementation.

  • Requirements definition and technical evaluation
  • Vendor comparison and solution review
  • Procurement, licensing and supplier coordination
  • Infrastructure upgrades and migrations
  • Implementation testing and handover follow-up
  • Third-party system and integration coordination
Best for businesses that need experienced technical ownership across projects, vendors and implementation decisions.
