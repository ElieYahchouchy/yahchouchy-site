When I Can Help

For businesses that need clearer IT ownership — not more complexity.

I am usually most useful when technology has become important enough to need senior oversight — whether you need fractional IT management, additional leadership for an existing IT team, or experienced coordination across vendors and technology projects.

01

02

03

04

A migration, infrastructure upgrade, cloud project, new system or vendor decision needs experienced technical ownership.
